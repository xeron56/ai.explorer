Diversification is the habit of refusing to let one forecast decide your financial future. A strong portfolio spreads exposure across asset classes, sectors, currencies, and time horizons.

## Start with the goal

Before choosing investments, define the job of the money. Emergency savings, a home down payment, retirement capital, and speculative capital should not share the same risk profile.

## Use broad building blocks

For many investors, the core can be built from low-cost funds:

- Broad U.S. equity exposure
- International equity exposure
- High-quality bonds or cash equivalents
- A small allocation to alternatives if appropriate

The exact mix depends on time horizon and tolerance for drawdowns.

## Rebalance with rules

Markets drift. Rebalancing once or twice a year keeps risk aligned with the original plan and forces a disciplined buy-low, sell-high behavior.
