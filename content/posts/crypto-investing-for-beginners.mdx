Crypto is a high-volatility asset class. It can offer meaningful upside, but it also carries technology, liquidity, regulatory, and custody risks that do not exist in the same way for traditional investments.

## Start small

Beginners should size positions so a large drawdown does not affect financial stability. Crypto should usually come after emergency savings and a diversified long-term portfolio.

## Understand custody

Exchange accounts are convenient, while self-custody gives more control and more responsibility. Losing keys can mean losing access permanently.

## Avoid narrative traps

Strong communities and fast price moves can create false confidence. Focus on liquidity, security, use case, and risk before chasing momentum.
