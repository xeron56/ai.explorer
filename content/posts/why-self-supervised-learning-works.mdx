An emergency fund is not idle money. It is insurance against being forced to sell investments or borrow at a bad time.

## Choose the right size

Three to six months of essential expenses is a practical starting range. Freelancers, single-income households, and people in cyclical industries may need a larger buffer.

## Keep it boring

The fund should be liquid, stable, and separate from daily spending. High-yield savings accounts, money market funds, or short Treasury bills can work depending on access needs.

## Build it automatically

Set a monthly transfer and treat it like a bill. Once the target is reached, new savings can flow toward long-term investments.
