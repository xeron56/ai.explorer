Interest rates are the price of money. When rates rise, borrowing becomes more expensive, future cash flows are discounted more heavily, and investors often demand higher returns for taking risk.

## Why stocks react

Growth companies are especially sensitive because much of their value comes from expected profits far in the future. Higher rates can compress valuations even when business performance remains healthy.

## What to watch

Investors should track inflation, central bank guidance, credit spreads, and the shape of the yield curve. Together, these signals show whether markets expect growth, slowdown, or stress.

Interest rates do not move every asset the same way, but they set the backdrop for nearly every investment decision.
