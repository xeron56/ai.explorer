## What this demo teaches

- How to read a single candlestick.
- The difference between reversal and continuation patterns.
- Why context matters more than any one candle.

## Example patterns

| Pattern | Meaning | Beginner signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hammer | Buyers reject lower prices | Watch for a bullish follow-through |
| Engulfing Bullish | Strong buying pressure after a down move | Consider a long bias if trend support holds |
| Doji | Market is undecided | Wait for the next candle before trading |
| Flag | Trend is pausing | Prepare for a breakout in the same direction |

## How to use the demo

Use this demo as a quick refresher before you move to the full guide. If a pattern appears without trend confirmation, treat it as a clue, not a trigger.
