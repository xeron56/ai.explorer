Swing trading sits between day trading and long-term investing. The goal is to capture multi-day or multi-week moves while keeping risk defined before the trade begins.

## Trade the trend

The simplest setup is buying pullbacks in an uptrend or shorting failed rallies in a downtrend. Moving averages, prior support, and volume confirmation can help filter weak setups.

## Define risk first

Every trade needs an invalidation point. If the setup depends on a support level holding, the stop should sit beyond that level with enough room for normal volatility.

## Keep the process narrow

Most swing traders do better with a focused watchlist, consistent setup rules, and smaller position sizes than with constant scanning and oversized conviction.
