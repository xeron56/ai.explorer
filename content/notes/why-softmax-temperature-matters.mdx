---
title: "Why Softmax Temperature Matters"
description: "A short note on temperature scaling and its effect on probabilities."
date: "2024-05-16"
category: "Theory"
---

The temperature $\tau$ in $\operatorname{softmax}(z/\tau)$ controls how *peaky*
the output distribution is.

$$
p_i = \frac{e^{z_i / \tau}}{\sum_j e^{z_j / \tau}}
$$

- As $\tau \to 0^+$, the distribution collapses to a one-hot at $\arg\max_i z_i$.
- As $\tau \to \infty$, the distribution becomes uniform.

A practical consequence: when knowledge-distilling from a large teacher, raise
$\tau$ on both teacher and student so the *dark knowledge* — the relative
ordering of the wrong classes — actually contributes to the cross-entropy.
