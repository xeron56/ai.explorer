Budgeting is not restriction for its own sake. It is a way to tell every dollar what job it should do before spending decisions get made for you.

## Start with cash flow

List fixed income, variable income, essential expenses, debt payments, and discretionary spending. The goal is to see the gap between what is earned and what is left over intentionally.

## Use a simple framework

- Needs
- Goals
- Wants

If goals do not get funded early, they usually get crowded out by low-priority spending later in the month.

## Improve gradually

Most good budgets get better through review. Track one month, find leaks, automate savings, and repeat until the plan feels realistic.
