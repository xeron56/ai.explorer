Finance is the process of deciding how money is earned, saved, invested, borrowed, and protected. At the individual level it helps people manage income and reach goals. At the business level it determines how companies fund growth and allocate capital.

## The three core questions

- How much money comes in?
- How much must be spent or reserved?
- Where should the remaining capital go?

## Why time matters

A dollar today can be saved, invested, or used to avoid debt. That makes present money more flexible than future money. This idea sits behind interest rates, discounting, and investing returns.

## What this chapter unlocks

Once the basics are clear, every later topic becomes easier to reason about: budgeting, investing, debt, valuation, and retirement planning all depend on the same foundation.
