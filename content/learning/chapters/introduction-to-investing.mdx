Investing means committing capital to assets that are expected to grow income or value over time. The edge for most people does not come from prediction. It comes from discipline, diversification, and time.

## The return engine

Returns often come from three sources:

- Earnings or income from the asset
- Growth in those earnings over time
- A change in how the market values the asset

## Compounding changes the game

Compounding turns small, repeated contributions into meaningful wealth. The earlier the process starts, the less return pressure is needed later.

## Focus on process

A sound plan beats a brilliant guess. Define goals, risk tolerance, contribution rate, and portfolio rules before selecting instruments.
