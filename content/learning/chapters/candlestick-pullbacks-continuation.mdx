After you identify trend direction, the next skill is reading pullbacks.

The screenshots label pullbacks as corrective moves. In a downtrend, these are the small upward bounces that appear between stronger selling waves. In an uptrend, they are the small downward dips that appear between stronger buying waves.

## Impulse versus correction

An impulse move is the stronger movement in the direction of the trend. It often has larger candles, fewer overlaps, and clearer closes.

A correction is the weaker movement against the trend. It often has smaller candles, more overlap, and slower progress.

In a downtrend, the impulse is down and the correction is up. In an uptrend, the impulse is up and the correction is down.

## What a healthy pullback looks like

A healthy pullback does not break the major swing structure.

In an uptrend, a pullback can dip, but it should ideally hold above the prior higher low. In a downtrend, a bounce can rise, but it should ideally fail below the prior lower high.

This is why pullbacks cannot be judged by color alone. A few green candles in a downtrend do not automatically mean reversal. They may only be temporary relief before sellers return.

## How candles reveal weak pullbacks

Weak pullbacks often show:

- Smaller candle bodies than the impulse move.
- More overlapping candles.
- Wicks that reject the counter-trend direction.
- Failure to close beyond the prior swing level.

These clues suggest the counter-trend side is active, but not strong enough to take control.

## When a pullback becomes a reversal warning

A pullback becomes more serious when it breaks structure.

In an uptrend, a pullback that makes a lower low is no longer a normal higher-low dip. In a downtrend, a bounce that makes a higher high is no longer a normal lower-high bounce.

That does not guarantee a full reversal, but it tells you the previous trend rhythm has changed.

## Beginner mistake to avoid

Do not enter only because price "pulled back." A pullback is a location, not a signal.

First define the trend. Then define the swing level that should hold. Then wait for candles to show whether momentum is returning in the trend direction or structure is breaking.
