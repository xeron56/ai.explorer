The stock market is a venue where ownership in companies changes hands. Buyers and sellers meet through exchanges, and prices move as expectations about growth, risk, and liquidity change.

## What a stock represents

A share is a small ownership claim on a business. If the company grows profits and allocates capital well, shareholders can benefit through price appreciation or dividends.

## Who moves the market

- Retail investors
- Institutions
- Market makers
- Long-term funds
- Short-term traders

Each group operates on a different time horizon, which is why markets can be both efficient and noisy.

## What beginners should watch

Price alone is not enough. Volume, earnings quality, balance-sheet strength, and valuation all matter when judging a stock.
