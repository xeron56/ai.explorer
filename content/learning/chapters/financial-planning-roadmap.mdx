Financial planning connects day-to-day money decisions with long-term outcomes. Instead of treating saving, debt, investing, and protection as separate tasks, it puts them into one roadmap.

## The planning order

1. Stabilize cash flow
2. Build emergency reserves
3. Protect against major risks
4. Eliminate toxic debt
5. Invest consistently for long-term goals

## What a good plan includes

Goal timelines, savings rates, asset allocation, insurance coverage, tax awareness, and a review schedule.

## Keep it adaptive

Life changes. A strong plan gets revised when income, family needs, or macro conditions change. The process should be stable even when the numbers move.
