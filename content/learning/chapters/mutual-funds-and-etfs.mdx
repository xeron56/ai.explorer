Mutual funds and ETFs both package many securities into one product. The difference is mainly in how they trade, how efficiently they handle taxes, and how investors access them.

## Mutual funds

Mutual funds trade once per day at end-of-day net asset value. They often support automatic investment plans and can be convenient for retirement accounts.

## ETFs

ETFs trade intraday like stocks. They often carry lower expense ratios and can be more tax efficient in taxable accounts.

## Choosing between them

The best choice depends on account type, platform features, cost, and your need for intraday liquidity. For long-term investors, behavior matters more than format.
