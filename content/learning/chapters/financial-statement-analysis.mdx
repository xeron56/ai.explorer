Financial statements tell the story of how a business earns money, spends money, and funds itself. Reading them well is a major step up from investing on headlines alone.

## The three statements

- Income statement: profitability over a period
- Balance sheet: assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time
- Cash-flow statement: how cash moved in operations, investing, and financing

## What strong analysis asks

Are margins stable? Is debt reasonable? Does profit convert to cash? Are management claims supported by the numbers?

## Avoid single-metric thinking

No ratio explains a company alone. Good analysis connects growth, profitability, balance-sheet strength, and valuation into one picture.
