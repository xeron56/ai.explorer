Derivatives are contracts whose value comes from an underlying asset, rate, or index. They can be useful tools, but they amplify both precision and mistakes.

## Common instruments

- Options
- Futures
- Forwards
- Swaps

## Legitimate uses

Airlines hedge fuel costs, exporters hedge currencies, and investors hedge portfolios. The structure is not inherently dangerous. Misuse is.

## Main risks

Leverage, time decay, counterparty exposure, and complexity can create losses that are much larger than beginners expect. If payoff diagrams are unclear, the position is probably premature.
