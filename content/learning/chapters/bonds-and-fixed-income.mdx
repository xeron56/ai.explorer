Fixed-income investing is about lending money in exchange for scheduled payments and principal repayment. The tradeoff is usually lower upside in exchange for more predictable cash flows.

## Key moving parts

- Coupon rate
- Maturity
- Credit quality
- Yield to maturity

These determine both the income stream and the risk profile.

## Why prices move

Bond prices and interest rates move in opposite directions. When new bonds offer higher yields, older lower-yield bonds become less attractive and their prices fall.

## Portfolio role

High-quality bonds can reduce volatility, fund short-term goals, and provide dry powder during equity drawdowns.
