Corporate finance is the discipline of deciding how a company funds operations and where it should invest its capital. The goal is not growth at any cost. The goal is value creation.

## Core decisions

- Invest in projects or acquisitions
- Raise debt or equity
- Return cash through dividends or buybacks
- Preserve liquidity and manage risk

## Good capital allocation

The best management teams compare expected return against cost of capital and opportunity cost. A project that grows revenue but earns weak returns can still destroy value.

## Investor takeaway

Studying capital allocation helps explain why two similar businesses can produce very different shareholder outcomes over time.
